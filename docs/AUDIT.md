# Audit of the data, October 2026

What was checked, what is wrong, how much it matters for the agent-based model of the GB day-ahead market and its
storage and renewables entry/exit layer, and what to do about it. Audited on 10 Oct 2026.

**Data audited.** The published processed pack (`dist/gb-power-data_processed_2026-10-05.zip`, built from commit
`dd86a2b-dirty`), which is byte-identical to the research repository's processed tables except `agents_{y}` and
`mid_target_{y}`; the raw snapshots the local configuration reads; this repository's processed ownership, register,
ancillary and storage tables; and the licensed N2EX and EPEX SPOT files. Results derived from the licensed files are
described here only in words: their numbers and failing dates are in the local report
(`data/processed/dayahead_audit_local.md`, never committed). Official figures come from
`data/reference/official_statistics.csv`, each with its source, locator and status.

**How to reproduce.** `python -m pytest tests/realdata` runs the checks below on whatever data the configuration
points at (skipped where absent; `-m "not licensed"` leaves out the licensed ones). Known open defects are strict
`xfail`s that name their ID here.

**Severity.** *critical*: the model gets a wrong input of material size; *major*: a number in a table is wrong or
missing in a way that changes a result; *minor*: a definition, label or edge case. **Effort**: S under half a day,
M one to three days, L more.
