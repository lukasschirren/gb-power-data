# Data gaps: open datasets to add

The open datasets that would complete the inputs of the agent-based model of the GB day-ahead market and its storage
and renewables entry/exit layer, each with where it is, its licence, how to get it, how often it changes and the
module that will own it. Checked on 10 Oct 2026 by fetching every page and file named here; a licence is quoted
from the page that states it. Anything not confirmed is marked **needs verification**, with the check that would
confirm it. No commercial source is listed: the model's licensed inputs remain the N2EX and EPEX SPOT
subscriptions, and Modo's public research notes stay an optional, anecdotal cross-check
([AUDIT.md](AUDIT.md), G-2).

**Licences used below.**

- **NESO ODL**: National Energy SO Open Data Licence v1.0, based on OGL v3 ("a worldwide, royalty-free, perpetual,
  non-exclusive licence"; attribution "Supported by National Energy SO Open Data"), for datasets on the NESO data
  portal. Documents on NESO's website (reports, workbooks) are under its site terms instead, which allow copies for
  own use only: cite them, do not pack them.
- **BMRS**: Elexon's BMRS data licence ("Contains BMRS data © Elexon Limited copyright and database right [year]").
- **OGL**: Open Government Licence v3.0 (gov.uk, LCCC's data portal, Ofgem's text, the UK Government Web Archive's
  copies of gov.uk files).
- **CC BY 4.0**: as stated by the publisher.

**Owners.** A puller is `gbpowerdata.pull.<x>` writing to `data/raw/`, a stage `gbpowerdata.build.<x>` writing to
`data/processed/`, a hand-curated table sits in `data/reference/` with a source and status on every value (as
`thermal_parameters.csv`). *New* marks a module to be written; the others exist.

## B1. Battery energy capacity (MWh), duration and dates per unit

No open source states MWh for every battery: DESNZ says it has "no reliable data source for energy capacity
figures" (Grid-scale battery storage statistics, 30 Jun 2026). `storage_fleet` combines
what exists; on 10 Oct 2026 it states MWh for 238 of 395 batteries (72% of their capacity; 131 before the MDV
submissions and the CM links by name and postcode of 10 Oct 2026; the twelve batteries added since come from the FFR
tenders of 2019-2023).
